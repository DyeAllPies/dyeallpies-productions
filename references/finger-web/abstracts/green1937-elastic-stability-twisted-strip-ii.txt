Secondhand description (Chopin & Kudrolli 2013, ref. [10], and Chopin, Démery & Davidovitch
2015 §1.1, both in papers/): Green's is the seminal analysis, later confirmed by numerical
simulation, showing that a ribbon held under tension is unstable to twist because twist produces
a spatially nonuniform compressive stress across the ribbon's width; his approach treats the
ribbon shape as close to a helicoid and derives the threshold at which that compressive stress
buckles the ribbon. This is Part II of a pair — Part I is "The equilibrium and elastic stability of a thin twisted
strip," Proc. R. Soc. A (1936), DOI 10.1098/rspa.1936.0061 (volume/page not confirmed this pass)
— also paywalled, not fetched.
