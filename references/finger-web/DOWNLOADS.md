## Item 02 — the twisted ribbon's regimes, and rods with contact — 2026-09-19

See `downloads-02.md`. 8 papers (`papers/`): three on the twisted-ribbon phase diagram (Chopin &
Kudrolli 2013 PRL, Chopin/Démery/Davidovitch 2015 J. Elasticity, Chopin & Kudrolli 2016 Soft
Matter) and five on position-based/projective-dynamics rods with contact (Kugelstadt & Schömer
2016, Soler/Martin/Sorkine-Hornung 2018, Umetani/Schmidt/Stam 2014, Bergou et al. 2008 Discrete
Elastic Rods, Bender/Müller/Macklin 2017 survey), plus a `.txt` mining pass added to the
already-archived `macklin2016-xpbd.pdf`. Numbers in
`data/02-twisted-ribbon-and-rod-contact.csv`. Note: `01-elastic-membrane-and-hole-formation.md`'s
brief gave arXiv ID 1306.1583 for Chopin & Kudrolli 2013 — that ID is wrong (resolves to an
unrelated astrophysics paper); the correct PDF was found on Arshad Kudrolli's own site instead.

Two items could not be obtained and are marked NOT FOUND in
`02-twisted-ribbon-regimes-and-rod-contact.md`: the full text of A. E. Green's 1937 "The elastic
stability of a thin twisted strip. II" (paywalled at the Royal Society, no open mirror), and
E. M. Mockensturm's 2001 "The Elastic Stability of Twisted Plates" (paywalled at ASME, no open
mirror). Both are described secondhand via their citations in the Chopin papers.
