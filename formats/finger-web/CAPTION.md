# Caption — the finger web, round five (the spectrum ribbon, the helicoid twist)

An elastic ribbon between my thumbs and index fingers. It only exists in the code: MediaPipe tracks the four fingertips, the ribbon is a sheet pinned to them, its long edges hang as exact catenaries when slack, and the strain field inside decides how thick it is and what shows through it. When I flip a hand the sheet twists half a turn on its own, because the model folds where the fingers reverse, and the edge nearer the camera passes in front. The colour is the strain in the order of the spectrum, on the sheet and on its edges alike: red where the ribbon is slackest, yellow at rest, cyan, blue and violet where it is stretched hardest. When I pinch, the ribbon is gathered into my fingers and the lines tighten until nothing is left at the touch. At the touch the ribbon fades out, and the flame that writes the logo rises from that point. Where a hand flips, the two edges are one twisted ribbon: the twist spreads along its length, the edges bow like a helicoid's and meet at the point where the ribbon is edge-on. The short edges between thumb and index read how far the fingers are spread, on the same scale. The logo at the end is written in the ribbon's own colours.

The screen recording around it is staged: the VS Code strip shows the real lines of the model that produced the render, and the panel on the right is drawn from the model's own numbers frame by frame, but the video was composed after the fact, not captured live.

Everything is in the open: the model, the renderer, the scene, the fire, the research and the numbers.
